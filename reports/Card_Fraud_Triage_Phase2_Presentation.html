2. Deep Learning Formulation: Non-Linear Autoencoders

Slide 3 / 10
1
The Manifold Hypothesis (Sakurada & Yairi 2014): Legitimate spending patterns concentrate along a smooth, low-dimensional non-linear manifold in 29-D space.
2
Informational Bottleneck: Squeezing 29 features down to 8 latent variables forces the network to discard noise and retain essential spending dynamics.
3
Anomaly Scoring: Unsupervised Reconstruction Error S_AE(x) = (1/d) ||x - x_hat||^2.
4
Normal Reconstruction: Familiar normal transactions reconstruct faithfully with negligible error (S_AE < 0.6).
5
Fraud Reconstruction Blowup: Novel fraudulent spending deviates orthogonally, causing reconstruction blowup (S_AE > 3.7).
Dual-Path Reconstruction Pipeline: Normal vs. Fraud

3. Funnel Architecture Design & Parameter Parity

Slide 4 / 10
1
Symmetric Funnel Topology: 29 → 20 → 12 → 8 (Bottleneck) → 12 → 20 → 29. Gradual compression preserves hierarchical correlations.
2
LeakyReLU Activation (α=0.1): Standardized input features have negative values; LeakyReLU avoids dead dying ReLU neurons and preserves gradient flow.
3
BatchNorm1d: Placed after each linear layer to eliminate internal covariate shift and accelerate convergence.
4
Linear Output (No Sigmoid): Standardized input dimensions span (-∞, +∞); a Sigmoid would artificially crush reconstruction variance.
5
Strict Parameter Parity: Exactly 2,061 trainable parameters vs. GMM's 1,394 parameters. Absolutely no unfair deep network parameter bloat!

4. Training Dynamics & Loss Convergence

Slide 5 / 10
1
Strict Isolation: Trained exclusively on 169,893 clean normal transactions (0 frauds) using the Adam optimizer.
2
Batch Size 256: Balances gradient smoothness with memory efficiency (664 batches per epoch).
3
Learning Rate Scheduling: Initial rate 10^-3 with ReduceLROnPlateau (factor 0.5, patience 3) for smooth fine-grained convergence.
4
Hardware Acceleration: Executed seamlessly on native Apple Silicon GPU (MPS), completing 40 epochs in under 3 minutes.
5
Generalization Confirmed: Train loss (0.575) and validation loss (0.574) track tightly without a hint of overfitting.

5. Controlled Ablations & The Tail-Noise Discovery

Slide 6 / 10
1
7-Model Grid: Systematically varied Bottleneck dimensions (4, 8, 14), Dropout (0.1, 0.2), Denoising noise, and Huber loss.
2
Champion Model: AE-Dropout0.2 achieved champion performance (Val AP = 0.7049, Val ROC = 0.9472).
3
The Viva Insight (Tail-Feature Noise): Raw Euclidean MSE treats all 29 dimensions equally. Minor tail PCA features (V28, V20) have noisy outliers that spike MSE and cause false alarms!
4
Why GMM Succeeded: GMM's full covariance matrix (Σ^-1) acts as a Mahalanobis distance, dividing each dimension by its variance (1 / σ_j^2).
5
Dropout as Noise Shield: 20% Dropout prevents the autoencoder from memorizing noisy tail coordinates.
Top KS Discriminant Features (V14, V12, V10) vs Noisy Tail Features

6. Innovation: GMM + Autoencoder Hybrid Fusion

Slide 7 / 10
1
Complementary Synergy: GMM excels at covariance noise dampening; Autoencoder excels at non-linear manifold curvature.
2
The Scale Incompatibility: GMM negative log-likelihood spans [0, 500,000+], while AE reconstruction MSE spans [0, 50]. Raw addition is impossible.
3
Percentile Rank Normalization: Converts both scores to uniform empirical percentiles: S_norm = Rank(S) / N ∈ [0, 1].
4
Linear Convex Combination: S_hybrid = α S_norm_GMM + (1 - α) S_norm_AE.
5
Zero Leakage: Blending weight α is calibrated strictly on the validation set, never touching the frozen test set.
Percentile Rank-Normalized Score Fusion Architecture
